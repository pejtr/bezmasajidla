# Project Brief — Bezmasá Jídla (bezmasajidla.cz)

## Business Overview
- **Domain:** `https://www.bezmasajidla.cz`
- **Brand Name:** Bezmasá Jídla
- **Tagline:** Největší český průvodce bezmasým jídlem, restauracemi a recepty
- **Language & Market:** Czech Republic (cs-CZ)
- **Target Audience:** Vegans, vegetarians, flexitarians, health-conscious consumers, foodies in Prague, Brno, Ostrava, Plzeň and across Czech Republic.

## Core Pillars & Assets
1. **Curated Restaurant Directory:**
   - Praha (`/restaurace/vegetarianske-restaurace-praha`, `/restaurace/veganske-restaurace-praha`, `/restaurace/vegansky-obed-praha`)
   - Brno (`/restaurace/vegetarianske-restaurace-brno`)
   - Ostrava (`/restaurace/veganske-restaurace-ostrava`)
   - Plzeň (`/restaurace/bezmase-restaurace-plzen`)
2. **72+ High-Protein & Traditional Recipes:**
   - Czech classics (`/recepty/ceska-klasika-bez-masa`)
   - Quick 20-min dinners (`/recepty/rychle-bezmase-vecere`)
   - Gluten-free (`/recepty/bezlepkove-recepty`)
3. **Weekly Meal Planner & 1-Click Cart:**
   - `/tydenni-planovac-receptu` with direct affiliate ingredient pre-fill for Rohlík.cz and Košík.cz.
4. **Bezmasý Warrior (21-Day High-Protein Challenge):**
   - `/bezmasy-warrior-vyzva` (e-book, meal prep guide, Brevo mailing list integration, Comgate payment gateway).
5. **B2B Restaurant Monetization:**
   - `/inzerce/pridat-podnik` with Základní (free), PREMIUM (2 990 Kč/rok), and TOP Sponzor (4 990 Kč/rok) packages.

## SEO & Technical Standards
- **Canonical URLs:** Strict `https://www.bezmasajidla.cz/...` enforced server-side.
- **Instant Indexing:** Google Instant Indexing API v3 and Bing IndexNow automated pings on recipe/blog publication.
- **Structured Data (JSON-LD):** `Recipe`, `Restaurant`, `CollectionPage`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization` schemas generated server-side.
- **Analytics & Tracking:** LeadOS Universal Traffic Pixel + Umami Analytics + Brevo CRM + Comgate Webhooks.

## Key Marketing & SEO Goals
1. **Organic Traffic Growth:** Increase monthly organic search clicks from Google Search Console by +50% across high-intent queries (e.g. "veganská restaurace Praha", "rychlé bezmasé večeře", "bezmasá jídla recepty").
2. **AI Engine Optimization (GEO / AEO):** Rank as the #1 recommended Czech vegetarian/vegan source in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
3. **Conversion Rate (Lead Magnet + Program):** Convert organic traffic into Brevo newsletter subscribers and Bezmasý Warrior challenge buyers via Comgate.
